# Concepts

The permanent layer. One idea per note, in your own words, linked to everything it touches.

1. **One idea per note.** If the title needs an "and", it's two notes.
2. **Title it as the idea**, not the topic.
3. **Own words.** Quoting means you haven't understood it yet.
4. **Always connect.** End with links to what it supports, contradicts, or depends on.

`/mine` proposes candidates from the week's notes; you pick which ones become permanent.
